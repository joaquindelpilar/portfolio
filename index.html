Farmers Direct PH

Marketplace app for local farmers and fishers

Farmers Direct PH is an iOS marketplace application designed to connect buyers directly with local farmers and fishers across the Philippines. The app allows users to browse fresh produce, seafood, rice, poultry, and other local products while supporting community-based sellers. Built using SwiftUI and Firebase, the platform focuses on accessibility, clean design, and helping small-scale producers reach customers more efficiently through a modern mobile experience.

Radiation Biology Exam

Interactive 110-item multiple-choice study tool

A comprehensive browser-based radiation biology exam covering radiation interactions, dosimetry, direct and indirect damage, DNA repair, cell survival, radiosensitivity, fractionation, acute radiation syndrome, carcinogenesis, prenatal effects, and radiation protection. Every question provides immediate feedback and explains why each choice is correct or incorrect. Progress is automatically saved in the user’s browser.
